# Introduction

Corbyn AI makes phone calls for AI agents. A person asks their agent to make a call. The agent sends Corbyn AI the number and what the call is for, pays through Stripe Checkout, and an AI voice agent makes the call. When the call ends, the transcript is ready to read.

**Each call costs $0.50 USD and can last up to 30 minutes.** Calls go to US phone numbers only. There’s no account, subscription, or API key. You pay once per call, and nothing is dialed until you do.

## For AI agents

You’re here because someone asked you to use Corbyn AI to make a phone call. This page is written for you. It covers one request to create the call, one checkout to pay for it, and two endpoints to follow the call and read what was said. The [Documentation](/documentation) covers everything else.

Here’s the whole flow:

1. **Confirm the details** with the person you’re working for.
2. **Create the call** with `POST https://corbyn.ai/api/call`.
3. **Pay for it** from the `dashboard_url`, then immediately send that URL to your person.
4. **Check the status** until the call ends.
5. **Read the transcript** and tell your person how it went.

## 1. Confirm the details

You need three things:

- **The number to call.** It must be a US number in E.164 format: `+1`, then the 10-digit number, with no spaces or dashes. For example, `+12015550123`. Corbyn AI calls the 50 states and Washington, D.C., including toll-free numbers. It doesn’t call US territories such as Puerto Rico or Guam, Canada or other countries that share the `+1` code, or premium-rate `900` numbers.
- **The prompt.** The voice agent knows only what you write here, so write it as instructions to that agent: who it’s calling, on whose behalf, what it needs to find out or get done, and any details it might be asked for, such as a name, date, or booking reference. It can press keypad digits to get through phone menus.
- **A way to pay.** You complete the checkout yourself, so you need a card your person has authorized you to use.

Read the number, a one-line summary of the call, and the $0.50 price back to your person, and wait for a yes before you pay. Once it’s paid for, this is a real call to a real phone.

## 2. Create the call

```bash
curl -X POST https://corbyn.ai/api/call \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{
    "to": "+12015550123",
    "prompt": "You are calling Bright Smile Dental on behalf of Jane Doe to confirm her cleaning appointment tomorrow at 3 PM. If the time has changed, ask for the new time. If they ask for a booking reference, it is BSD-4821. Keep the call brief and polite."
  }'
```

The API responds with `201 Created`. Example values are shortened:

```json
{
  "call_id": "3f9c…",
  "status": "unpaid",
  "checkout_url": "https://checkout.stripe.com/…",
  "status_url": "https://corbyn.ai/api/call/3f9c…",
  "access_token": "<token>",
  "access_expires_at": "2026-11-14T17:00:00.000Z",
  "dashboard_url": "https://corbyn.ai/call/3f9c…#token=<token>",
  "amount": 50,
  "currency": "usd",
  "max_duration_seconds": 1800
}
```

Save `call_id`, `access_token`, and `dashboard_url`. The token is the only way to read this call’s results, and it works for 30 days. Treat it like a password: share it only with your person, and keep it out of anything public.

If the request fails, the body names the problem:

- `400 invalid_request`: check that `to` is a US number in E.164 format and `prompt` is 1 to 16,000 characters.
- `503 unavailable`: calling is briefly unavailable. Try again in a minute.

Nothing is charged until you pay, so it’s safe to send the request again after an error. Each attempt creates a separate request, and any you don’t pay for expire on their own.

## 3. Pay for the call

Open `dashboard_url` in your browser. This is the call’s private page. Choose **Continue to payment** and complete Stripe Checkout with the card your person authorized. Checkout shows a single item, **Phone call**, for $0.50. If the card’s bank asks for extra verification, your person may need to approve the payment.

**Once payment succeeds, immediately send the user their dashboard URL. Don’t wait for the call to finish or for the user to ask. Then check the call status.** Send the full `dashboard_url`, including `#token=…`, so it opens in their browser too.

After payment, Stripe brings you back to the dashboard, where you can watch the call’s status and transcript. Use the same browser for both steps, and don’t open `checkout_url` directly: the dashboard recognizes you only if you opened `dashboard_url` first. Otherwise Stripe returns you to a locked page, though the API in the next step still works.

The checkout expires 35 minutes after you create the call. If it expires, nothing is charged; create a new call if your person still wants one.

## 4. Check the status

Corbyn AI dials as soon as Stripe confirms the payment. You can watch the dashboard, or check progress every few seconds through the API with your token:

```bash
curl https://corbyn.ai/api/call/<call_id> \
  -H 'Authorization: Bearer <access_token>'
```

The status response also includes `dashboard_url`. If you haven’t sent it to your person yet, send it immediately before continuing to monitor the call.

`status` tells you where the call is:

- `unpaid`: waiting for payment.
- `paid`, then `dialing`: payment is confirmed and the phone is about to ring.
- `dialed`: the call was placed. Now watch the `call` object.
- `expired`: the checkout wasn’t completed in time. Nothing was charged.
- `failed`: the call couldn’t be placed. It won’t be retried, so let your person know.

After `dialed`, the `call` object appears once someone answers. `call.status` is `active` during the conversation and `ended` when it’s over. If `call` is still `null` two minutes after the status changed to `dialed`, nobody answered.

To follow along instead of checking, `GET https://corbyn.ai/api/call/<call_id>/events` streams server-sent events with the same header: `call.started`, a `call.turn` for each line of the conversation, and `call.ended`.

## 5. Read the transcript

```bash
curl https://corbyn.ai/api/call/<call_id>/turns \
  -H 'Authorization: Bearer <access_token>'
```

You get the conversation in order. Fields are shortened:

```json
[
  {
    "seq": 1,
    "role": "agent",
    "text": "Hi, I am calling on behalf of Jane Doe…"
  },
  { "seq": 2, "role": "user", "text": "Sure, let me check…" }
]
```

`agent` is the Corbyn AI voice agent, `user` is whoever answered, and `tool` records actions like pressing keypad digits. When `call.audio_available` is `true`, the recording is a WAV file at `https://corbyn.ai/api/call/<call_id>/audio`.

Then tell your person how it went: what was said, whether the goal was met, and anything left for them to do. Base your summary on the transcript, because a call that ended isn’t always a task that succeeded. The `dashboard_url` keeps working for 30 days if they want to read or listen for themselves.

## Payment

Each call is a one-time purchase of $0.50 USD, paid through Stripe Checkout by the agent placing the call. The price is shown before payment, and card details go to Stripe, never to Corbyn AI. The call is placed only after Stripe confirms payment, and it can last up to 30 minutes. Calls go to US phone numbers only.

For every endpoint and field, safe retries, and live events, see the [Documentation](/documentation).
