## 1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Corbyn AI is operated by Ayush Kumar ("Corbyn AI," "we," "us," or "our") in Texas, United States, through [corbyn.ai](https://corbyn.ai). This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit our website, request or pay for a call, participate in a call, access call results, or contact us.

Puerto Rico, other US territories, Canada, and other international destinations are excluded. This service restriction does not remove privacy rights that otherwise apply to a visitor or person whose information we process.

A call can contain information about both the person requesting it and the people participating in it. If someone uses Corbyn AI to contact you, we may process your telephone number, speech, and other information shared during the conversation even if you have never visited our website.

This policy describes the hosted Corbyn AI service. Someone independently operating the software on their own infrastructure is responsible for explaining their own practices.

## 2. Information we collect

**Call requests.** We receive the destination telephone number, your instructions or prompt, and any information you include in those instructions. Requests may be saved before payment is completed.

**Call content and results.** Calls made through Corbyn AI are recorded and transcribed. We process call audio to run the AI conversation and store the audio recording, transcript, and call metadata, such as telephone numbers, direction, timing, duration, call identifiers, and status. Transcripts and recordings may contain personal information that participants choose to disclose or that a customer supplies in advance.

**Calling and recording permissions.** We require the customer requesting a call to inform the intended participants about the AI voice, recording, transcription, and sharing of results with the customer, and to obtain all required permissions before the call begins. Corbyn AI does not currently provide an automatic audible recording announcement or an in-call consent prompt. A customer's agreement to our [Terms and Conditions](/terms-and-conditions) or payment does not constitute consent on behalf of another participant, and this allocation of responsibility does not remove Corbyn AI's own obligations under applicable law.

**Purchases.** Stripe collects payment details through its checkout service. We receive payment status, amount, currency, payment and customer identifiers, and contact details provided through checkout, which may include name, email address, and telephone number. Corbyn AI's application does not store full payment-card numbers or card security codes.

**Access information.** We maintain a verifier for each call's access token, its expiration, and any revocation state. If a creation-recovery secret is used, we retain a verifier and related recovery information. We use this information to check access and handle authorized retries. Raw access tokens and recovery secrets are not stored in Corbyn AI's application database.

**Website and service interactions.** Operating the website and API involves processing technical information such as IP addresses, request information, browser information, and errors. Our infrastructure and service providers may maintain logs for operation, security, and troubleshooting. Browser storage and cookies are described below.

**Support.** If you contact us, we receive your email address, correspondence, and any information you provide to help resolve the request.

**Eligibility and permissions.** We may request information to confirm that a customer or destination is eligible for the Service, or evidence of calling and recording permissions to investigate a complaint or suspected misuse. Provide only what is needed for the request.

## 5. Cookies and browser storage

When you open a private call link, Corbyn AI uses its token to establish access and sets an essential cookie for that call. This cookie lets the browser retrieve call status, transcripts, audio, and live updates without requiring you to re-enter the token. It expires with the call's access grant or may become unusable if access is revoked.

The call-access cookie is HttpOnly and uses the Secure attribute in production. The private dashboard removes the token from the address bar after reading the link. The application does not put call tokens into localStorage or URL query parameters. A display preference, such as light or dark mode, may be stored locally in your browser.

You can clear or block cookies using your browser settings, but doing so may prevent private call pages from working until you reopen a valid private link. Stripe may use its own cookies and similar technologies on its checkout service.

## 6. Call access and stored information

You can access a call's results for 30 days from the date the call request is created, unless access is revoked sooner under our Terms. After 30 days, your access expires. Expiration of access does not automatically delete the associated call records.

We do not commit to a fixed deletion deadline for stored information. We retain personal information as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, taking into account service delivery, support, security, disputes, and legal and accounting obligations, subject to applicable law and privacy rights. The 30-day access window does not determine how long information is stored by Corbyn AI or its providers.

We may retain qualifying deidentified or aggregate information for ongoing lawful research, product development, model development, and commercial licensing, including after the underlying identifiable call records are deleted, provided the information continues to meet the standards described in Section 3. This does not extend the retention of identifiable source records or remove obligations that apply to them.

You can request deletion by emailing [ayush@corbyn.ai](mailto:ayush@corbyn.ai). We may need to verify the request and retain information required for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, or disputes. Where deletion is appropriate, we will address information held by relevant providers and in backups as required.

Where information has been deidentified so that we can no longer link it to you, we may be unable to locate your contribution within it. We do not reidentify people solely to associate such information with a request. This is not a blanket exemption for training datasets or models that still contain or reveal personal information, and applicable legal obligations continue to apply.

## 7. Access and security

Access to a call's results requires a valid credential for that call. The API checks whether the credential matches the call, has expired, or has been revoked. A token for one call does not grant access to other calls. Private responses use controls designed to prevent ordinary caching, and private pages discourage search indexing and transmission of referrer information.

These controls do not guarantee absolute security. Anyone who obtains a valid private link or token may be able to view the associated results. Avoid putting private links in public messages, analytics, or shared documents, and contact us if you believe one has been exposed.
